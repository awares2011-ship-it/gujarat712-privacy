Privacy Policy

Gujarat 7/12 & 8A Land Info · Last updated: 11 October 2026

Not a government app

Gujarat 7/12 & 8A Land Info is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the Government of Gujarat or its Revenue Department. Land records are fetched from the public website anyror.gujarat.gov.in. Records shown are for information only and are not certified copies.

What we collect

We do not run any server that receives your data. The app has no login, no ads and no analytics.

  • Land record searches (district, taluka, village, survey number and the captcha you type) are sent directly from your phone to anyror.gujarat.gov.in to fetch the record you asked for. We never receive them.
  • Search history, selected district and settings are stored only on your device. You can clear the history in the app (History → Clear) or by clearing the app's data.
  • PDF / PNG files you export are saved on your device and shared only when you choose to share them.

Third-party services contacted by the app

  • anyror.gujarat.gov.in – Revenue Department, Government of Gujarat (land records).
  • api.open-meteo.com – weather forecast for the district you choose (only the district's coordinates are sent).
  • news.google.com – agriculture news headlines; articles open on the publisher's website.

These services receive your device's IP address as part of any normal internet request and are governed by their own privacy policies.

Notifications

If you turn on lock-screen alerts, the app checks the weather and news for your district in the background on your phone and shows local notifications. No notification data passes through our servers. You can turn alerts off in the app or in Android settings at any time.

Permissions

  • Internet – to fetch records, weather and news.
  • Notifications – optional, for weather and news alerts.
  • Storage (Android 9 and older only) – to save exported PDF/PNG files.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.
